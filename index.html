Calendar Agent

A personal project, not a public service.

Calendar Agent is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own Mac. It helps its owner manage their own Google Calendar by chatting, either in the Mac's Terminal or through a private Telegram bot that only answers its owner.

What it does

  • Shows upcoming events and finds free time.
  • Creates, moves and cancels events, including repeating ones.
  • Plans study sessions around existing commitments.
  • Sends its owner a short summary of the day each morning.

It always asks its owner before changing anything in the calendar.

Calendar Agent isn't offered to anyone else. Only its owner uses it.
